# Demo 03: Personal Finance App

## Brief

一个个人现金流规划 app，帮助自由职业者看清未来三个月的收支、税金和安全垫。场景敏感，用户可能焦虑，不能过度玩笑。

## Before

### Hero

Take control of your money.

### Empty State

No transactions. Connect your bank account.

### Warning

Low balance warning.

### Success

Budget created.

## Selected Direction: Harbor Ledger

### North Star

The user is navigating variable income waters; the product is a calm harbor ledger that helps them see currents, reserves, and safe passages.

### Expression Map

| Surface | Before | After | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hero | Take control of your money. | See the next three months before the tide changes. | Less domination, more foresight |
| Primary CTA | Connect bank | Connect accounts | Sensitive action stays plain |
| Empty state | No transactions. Connect your bank account. | Your ledger is ready. Connect an account to chart the first route. | Gentle metaphor, still clear |
| Warning | Low balance warning. | Your projected cushion drops below 14 days in late July. | Plain, specific, respectful |
| Success | Budget created. | Your July route is mapped. | Reinforces planning without over-celebration |
| Reminder | Add your income | Add expected income when you have a moment, so the forecast stays useful. | No guilt, no false intimacy |

## What Improved

- The product feels calmer and more premium without hiding serious information.
- Warnings remain explicit and actionable.
- The narrative supports long-term care through forecast, cushion, route, harbor, and tide metaphors.

## Guardrail

Financial risk states must prioritize clarity. Never replace exact amounts, dates, or actions with metaphor.
