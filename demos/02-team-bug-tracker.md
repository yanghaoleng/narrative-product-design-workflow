# Demo 02: Team Bug Tracker

## Brief

一个给小团队用的 bug tracker。目标不是做复杂企业系统，而是让团队快速看清问题、分派任务、修复并复盘。

## Selected Direction: Repair Bay

### North Star

The team is a repair crew keeping a product in working condition; the tracker is the bay wall that turns scattered faults into visible repair paths.

### Expression Map

| Surface | Before | After | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hero | Track bugs faster. | Bring every fault into the repair bay. | Turns tracking into shared action |
| Primary CTA | New issue | Log a fault | Concrete and still clear |
| Empty state | No issues found. | The bay is clear. New signals will land here. | Keeps calm, avoids false celebration |
| Assignment | Assign issue | Hand off repair | Emphasizes ownership |
| In progress | In progress | On the bench | Simple status metaphor |
| Resolved | Issue closed. | Repair verified. | More precise than "closed" |
| Weekly digest | 12 issues closed | 12 repairs cleared, 3 patterns worth inspecting | Encourages learning, not just throughput |

## What Improved

- The core workflow now has a physical shared space.
- Team language supports collaboration without turning bugs into enemies.
- The metaphor can extend to triage, repair, verification, recurring faults, and maintenance windows.

## Guardrail

Avoid militarized language such as "kill bugs" or "war room" unless the team's brand already uses it. Repair language keeps accountability without aggression.
