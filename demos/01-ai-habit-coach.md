# Demo 01: AI Habit Coach

## Brief

一个 AI 习惯教练，帮助用户建立轻量但长期的阅读、运动、睡眠习惯。用户容易半途而废，不喜欢被说教。

## Before

### Hero

Build better habits with AI.

### Empty State

No habits yet. Create your first habit.

### Missed Day

You missed your habit today. Try again tomorrow.

### Milestone

Congratulations! You completed a 7-day streak.

## Narrative Territories

| Territory | User role | Product role | Score | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Small Garden | Gardener | Quiet caretaker | 35 | Gentle, long-term, avoids shame |
| Training Camp | Athlete | Coach | 28 | Clear but too performance-heavy |
| Personal Quest | Explorer | Map keeper | 31 | Memorable but could overdramatize small habits |
| Workshop Bench | Craftsperson | Bench assistant | 30 | Practical but less emotionally warm |

## Selected Direction: Small Garden

### North Star

The user is tending a small living garden of daily practices; the product quietly helps water, prune, and protect growth without shaming missed days.

### Expression Map

| Surface | Before | After | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hero | Build better habits with AI. | Grow small rituals that can survive real life. | Shifts from productivity pressure to humane continuity |
| Primary CTA | Create habit | Plant a ritual | Keeps metaphor light and understandable |
| Empty state | No habits yet. Create your first habit. | This patch is still open. Plant one ritual to begin. | Gives first-step invitation without saying "empty" |
| Loading | Loading... | Checking today's soil... | Soft wait-state metaphor |
| Missed day | You missed your habit today. Try again tomorrow. | Today got crowded. Your ritual is still here when you return. | Removes guilt while preserving continuity |
| 7-day milestone | Congratulations! You completed a 7-day streak. | Seven days of care. Something is taking root. | Makes achievement specific and emotionally resonant |
| Reminder | Complete your habit now. | A small watering would keep today's ritual alive. | Gentle, not manipulative |

## What Improved

- The product now has a repeatable metaphor.
- Failure language is more respectful.
- Long-term retention motive becomes care, not streak anxiety.
- The setting can extend to seasons, pruning, seeds, beds, and weather without becoming obscure.

## Guardrail

Do not use garden metaphors for medical, financial, or legal failure states. In sensitive moments, use plain language first.
